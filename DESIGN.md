## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a culinary world of high-octane street craft: decadent, nocturnal, unapologetic, and curated. It bridges late-night urban food culture with editorial hospitality design. The interface does not mimic a fast-food counter; instead, it establishes an elevated urban gastronomy experience where deep nocturnal plum canvases meet razor-sharp golden typography, tactile stickers, and warm cream accents.

### Design Movement: Dark Editorial Street Gastronomy
The system fuses the raw kinetic vitality of street food posters with the refined discipline of high-end editorial layouts. 
- **Atmospheric Contrast:** Immersive obsidian-plum backgrounds replace generic dark grays, evoking the neon-lit velvet atmosphere of a late-night culinary haunt.
- **Editorial Typography:** Geometric and structural letterforms set in tight uppercase tracking provide immediate presence without cartoonish tropes.
- **Tactile Details:** Starburst micro-badges, angled sticker-style tags, and luminous hairline borders offer high tactile reward while maintaining strict structural clarity.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around a dark culinary atmosphere where warm radiant accents leap out from deep fruitwood-smoked plum tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FFC83D`):** The signature crisp golden yellow. Used for primary CTAs, key pricing elements, active states, and high-impact typographic callouts. Never used for massive body copy blocks; reserved for focal punch points.
- **Secondary (`#2D122B`):** A deep, saturated plum. Serves as structural containment, surface differentiation, and pill containers that soften raw contrast against the darker base.
- **Tertiary (`#F15A32`):** Street crimson/red-orange. Expresses heat, urgency, spicy badges, dietary callouts, and limited-edition stickers.
- **Neutral (`#1A0D18`):** The foundational night canvas. Infused with plum undertones rather than neutral zinc or cold slate, yielding a warm, appetizing background depth.

### Functional Roles & Layering
1. **Background Layer (`plum-obsidian` / `#140C13`):** Canvas backdrop and outer margins.
2. **Surface Layer (`plum-base` / `#1A0D18`):** Base cards, modular layout panels.
3. **Elevated Surfaces (`plum-elevated` / `#241622`):** Interactive card hovers, drawers, active trays, and dropdowns.
4. **Text Primary (`cream-surface` / `#FFF7E9`):** Soft high-contrast text avoiding the eye fatigue of pure `#FFFFFF` against dark plum.
5. **Text Secondary (`#B89FA5`):** Muted plum-gray for descriptive copy and metadata.

## Typography

Typography embodies a tension between the bold swagger of street posters and modern app clarity.

- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Set heavy (700–900). Epilogue provides architectural, condensed-geometric authority. Hero titles and major section headers should frequently employ `uppercase` styling with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic raw street-poster typography.
- **Body & Product Text (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides frictionless, contemporary legibility for descriptions, ingredients, prices, and complex ordering flows.
- **Micro-Badges & Stickers:** Styled exclusively using `label-badge` in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em`), delivering the punchy cadence of price stickers and flavor seals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded on a tight, rhythmic fluid column framework balanced by bold, billboard-style horizontal band sections.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` exterior canvas margins. Maximum container width is `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins.

### Spatial Cadence
- **Dense Card Interiors:** Street food menus thrive on density. Maintain `space-md` (`1rem`) internal padding on product tiles to preserve compact, appetizing scannability.
- **Editorial Section Breaks:** Alternate dense item grids with dramatic billboard rows utilizing `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to `4rem` vertical breathing room.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Hero showcases and category tickers can break grid gutters slightly (`-0.5rem` negative margins on mobile horizontal scrolls) to emphasize energetic velocity.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than conventional cloud-like drop shadows, elevation is achieved through **tonal stacking, sharp luminous contours, and ambient warm backlight glows**.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#140C13`. Non-interactive backdrop.
2. **Level 1 (Card / Container):** Flat fill of `#1A0D18` bordered by a `1px` subtle rim in `#3D1E3A`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Tile):** Shift to `#241622` with a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 200, 61, 0.3)`. An ambient golden backlight halo appears: `0px 8px 24px rgba(255, 200, 61, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modal / Floating Cart / Sticky Sheet):** Background of `#241622` surrounded by a `1px` border in `#F15A32` or `#FFC83D`, casting a deep nocturnal shadow: `0px 16px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Street Texture & Vignette
Use subtle 180-degree linear gradients that terminate in rich plum-black at the foot of cards and panels, creating organic rim lighting reminiscent of food truck neon beneath heat lamps.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tight, architectural corner radius of `0.25rem` (`roundedness: 1`), keeping elements crisp, precise, and graphic.

### Corner Treatment
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Standard Cards:** `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`). This maintains a sharp, editorial edge that prevents the UI from becoming childish or cartoonish.
- **Containers & Modal Windows:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`). Provides subtle structural containment without soft ballooning.
- **Product Badges & Micro-Stickers:** Angular or sheared elements (rotations of `-2deg` or `+3deg`) with `0.125rem` to `0.25rem` radius, simulating physically stamped street labels.
- **Cart Count & Pill Badges:** Full pill-shapes are strictly restricted to numeric notification capsules and toggle chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#FFC83D` with `#140C13` text. Weight `700`, uppercase, `0.25rem` radius. Subtle inner golden bevel. Hover state triggers a `1px` border shift to `#FFF7E9` with a subtle translate-y `(-1px)` jump.
- **Secondary (Heat):** Filled in `#F15A32` with `#FFF7E9` text. For limited deals and high-heat promotions.
- **Outline (Ghost):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #3D1E3A`, text `#FFF7E9`. On hover, border illuminates to `#FFC83D` with text shifting to `#FFC83D`.

### Menu & Product Cards
- **Structure:** Encased in `#1A0D18` with `1px solid #3D1E3A`.
- **Media Presentation:** High-contrast photography fills the upper aspect ratio (`4:3` or `1:1`) with a bottom fade into plum.
- **Action Strip:** Clear typography layout with the price highlighted in `#FFC83D` (`Epilogue 800`) beside a compact `+` button in `#2D122B` that expands on interaction.

### Badges & Street Stickers
- **Flavor & Spice Badges:** Angular tag with background `#F15A32`, text `#FFF7E9`, tracked wide, uppercase `0.75rem`.
- **Value & Special Flags:** Starburst or high-angled tags positioned over card corners using `#FFC83D` with `#140C13` bold text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Canvas:** Dark surface `#140C13` surrounded by `1px solid #3D1E3A`. Text rendered in `#FFF7E9`, placeholder text in `#7E636F`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions crisply to `#FFC83D` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 1px #FFC83D`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Square with `0.125rem` radius, `#140C13` surface, `#3D1E3A` border. Checked state fills with `#FFC83D` and displays a bold `#140C13` checkmark.
- **Segmented Filter Bar:** Horizontal track with `#140C13` background. Active tab pill renders in `#2D122B` with a `#FFC83D` accent line or active pill fill.
